# Orbio Earth

> Orbio Earth is a climate-tech company that turns open satellite data into industrial methane
> intelligence for the energy sector. Its platform detects, quantifies, and tracks methane emissions
> from oil-and-gas infrastructure globally at asset level (~5-day revisit) using Sentinel-2, Landsat
> 8/9, and EMIT imagery plus machine learning and Bayesian uncertainty estimation. Access is via the
> web application or a sales-gated data API integration; there is no public self-serve developer
> portal, OpenAPI, or SDK at this time.
